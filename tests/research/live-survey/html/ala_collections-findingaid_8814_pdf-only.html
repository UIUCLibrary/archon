Scope and Contents of the Materials

Subject File (1983-84, 1994-2019) of the Office for Library Advocacy (OLA), including articles, audiovisual media, bookmarks, buttons, contracts and agreements, correspondence, emails, grants, handbooks, invoices, meeting minutes, posters, publications, reports, stickers, and toolkits, concerning OLA advocacy activities such as Advocacy Bootcamp, Advocacy Institutes, Advocacy University, ALA Advocacy Strategic Plans, Banned Books Week, Campaign for America's Libraries/Librarians, the Council of State Library Agencies in the Northeast (COSLINE), the Elementary and Secondary Education Act, "I Love Libraries," the Libraries on the Information Superhighway Advocacy Network (LISAN), Library Advocacy Now!,the Library Services and Technology Act, the National Book Festival, National Library Legislative Day, "Our Authors, Our Advocates," "Quotable Facts About America's Libraries," the School Library Journal, the School Library Task Force, the White House Conference on Library and Information Services Taskforce (WHCLIST), and the World Book-ALA Goal Award.
